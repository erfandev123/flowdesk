## Notes for resellers

**White-label.** Nothing in the code ties the product to the original author.
Rebrand it freely — the logo initials, colours, currency and locale are all
runtime configuration, not hard-coded.

**Attribution.** You are not required to credit the original author in your
product's user interface. Many resellers add a small "powered by" line for
goodwill, but it is entirely your choice.

**Support liability.** When you resell, the support promise you make to your
customer is yours to keep. This is the main practical reason to sell
white-label with a support contract rather than bare source code.

**Demo data.** The seeded demo workspace contains fictional companies and
contact details only. No real customer data is included anywhere in this
archive.
